## Interview Pitch

Arbiter answers the bank ledger design question directly. A payment does not mutate two balances independently. It creates a debit leg and a credit leg in one atomic write path, and those signed journal entries must sum to zero. The ledger is append-only, replayable from disk, protected by checksums, and reconciled after stress tests. Duplicate requests reuse the ISO-style `EndToEndId` as the idempotency key, so replayed payment messages cannot create additional journal entries.
